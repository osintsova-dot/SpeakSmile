1Writing Part 7: picture story

What is Part 7? There is a story writing task.Student’s Book стр. 127. Новое задание A2 Key: рассказ по трём картинкам, 35 слов и больше.

iExam information

What is Part 7? There is a story writing task.
What do I have to do? You have to write a story based on three pictures.

✓Exam tips

• Look at all three pictures and get an idea of the whole story before you begin.
• Include something about every picture in your story.
• Use the past tense for the events of the story.
• Try to use linking words such as so, and, but, because, etc. in your story.

По-русски: сначала посмотри на все три картинки и пойми историю целиком. В рассказе должно быть что-то про каждую картинку. Рассказ — в прошедшем времени от первого слова до последнего. Соединяй предложения словами so, and, but, because, when.

1Write your story

📄 Model answer (Teacher’s Book)
Last month Charlie saw a poster at school about a music competition. She loves playing the guitar, so she decided to enter it. She went home and practised a lot. Her family put their hands over their ears because they thought she sounded terrible. However, when she played on stage in the competition, everyone thought she was amazing. Charlie had a great night.

1Listening Part 2: gap fill notes

There is a person giving some information on a subject. You need to listen and complete some notes.Стр. 129. Five pieces of information: words, numbers or spellings.

✓Exam tips

• Before you listen, think about the kind of information you need for the gap.
• Sometimes, you will hear two possible answers. Listen carefully to understand which one is correct.
• It’s best to write numbers as numbers and not as words, so you don’t make a mistake with spelling.
• You hear the recording twice, so don’t worry if you miss the answer the first time.

Упр. 1. Read the exam task. Then, listen to the first part and look at the example. You hear two days — Saturday and Friday. Why is Saturday correct and Friday wrong?
