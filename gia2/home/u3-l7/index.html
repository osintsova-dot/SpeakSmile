Traditional costumes in BOLIVIA
Different regions of Bolivia have different costumes. Traditional clothes often tell us what region someone comes from.
The Highlands are a region in the west of Bolivia. The traditional costume there has a lot of colours. People wear it for festivals and on special occasions.
Men usually wear a red jacket, a white shirt, white trousers and white shoes. They often wear a hat.
Women have very colourful costumes. They wear long pink, yellow or blue skirts and a hat, and they sometimes wear boots.
In these photos, men are playing instruments and women are dancing in the street during the Laja Festival in La Paz. They are wearing traditional clothes.
